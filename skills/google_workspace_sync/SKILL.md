# Google Workspace Sync Skill

## Capabilities

### 1. Google Calendar (`createGoogleCalendarEvent`, `listGoogleCalendarEvents`)
- Creates personalized campus tour appointments for Kota doctor & coaching families.
- Schedules mandatory 60-day launch milestones (Rajasthan Fire NOC, Kota Stone Polishing, Teacher Auditions).
- Integrates pop-up and email reminders 30 mins before parents arrive.

### 2. Google Tasks (`createGoogleTaskItem`, `listGoogleTasks`)
- Synchronizes individual items from the 500-task launch library into co-founders' personal task lists.
- Segregates Academics tasks (for Priya) and Business & Facility tasks (for Ananya).

### 3. Google Drive (`createGoogleDriveDocument`, `listGoogleDriveFiles`)
- Uploads and indexes Canadian curriculum PDFs, vendor contracts, lease agreements, and staff police verification certificates.
